Product Overview & Showcase

The Smart, Adaptive Study Roadmap for DSA Aspirants

Most engineers abandon static DSA spreadsheets because life happens. Planli eliminates rigid planning by dynamically grouping problems into weekly milestone sprints, accommodating day-offs, and shifting pending questions forward in real time.

Students can switch between, customize, and maintain multiple concurrent roadmaps:

⚡ Striver 180 Sheet 179 Problems · 3,938 Est. Minutes

Curated SDE Sheet targeting high-frequency interview patterns and core algorithmic templates.

📚 Striver A-Z DSA Course 496 Problems · 11,518 Est. Minutes

End-to-end master curriculum covering basics, arrays, dynamic programming, trees, and advanced graphs.

Core Engine & Product Features

📅 Dynamic Sprint Scheduling

Enter your daily study capacity (e.g. 1.5h, 2.2h, 3h). Planli automatically partitions the curriculum into balanced 7-day sprints, predicting your target completion date accurately.

🏖️ Adaptive Day-Offs & Pauses

Taking a break or feeling unwell? Mark rest days and the engine automatically pushes pending work forward without splitting problems or disrupting already-completed tasks.

☁️ Real-Time Cloud Sync

Built on Google Cloud Firestore with IndexedDB offline fallback. Your progress, notes, and schedules sync instantly across your laptop, iPad, and mobile phone.

🔐 Frictionless Multi-Mode Auth

Sign in instantly with 1-click Google OAuth, Username/Email & Password, or verified phone options with zero friction.

📝 Problem Notes & Revision Bookmarks

Attach custom approach notes, time complexity breakdowns, and bookmark difficult problems for spaced repetition review.

🎨 Minimalist Dark-Mode UI

Designed for prolonged study sessions with distraction-free typography, quick question filtering, and direct links to takeUforward practice pages.
